Roasted Pumpkin Coconut Curry Soup

Everything roasts in one pot, then simmers with broth and coconut milk. The margins make it milder and creamier: one teaspoon of curry paste and a full can of coconut milk.

  • Printed recipe, Chris Scheuer
  • Serves 8
  • 425°F
  • About 50 minutes

Ingredients

To roast

  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 medium apple, cored and roughly chopped — don’t peel
  • 1 large potato, roughly chopped — don’t peel
  • 3 + 3 medium cloves garlic (the printout lists garlic twice)
  • 1 full teaspoon red curry paste
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon mild yellow curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • ½ teaspoon cumin
  • 2 teaspoons finely chopped fresh ginger

To finish

  • 1⅞ cups (30 tbsp) pumpkin purée — one 15-ounce can
  • 3 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • One full 400 ml can light coconut milk

Garnish

  • Coconut
  • Salted, roasted cashews, if desired
  • Greek yogurt thinned with a bit of milk, if desired

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 425°F.

  2. 02

    Combine the olive oil, butter, onion, apple, potato, garlic, curry paste, brown sugar, curry powder, coriander, cumin, and ginger in a Dutch oven or oven-safe pot. Stir well and roast in the preheated oven. (The roasting time is cut off on our copy.)

  3. 03

    Remove from the oven and stir in the pumpkin purée. Roast for another 15–20 minutes, until deepening in colour, stirring halfway through.

  4. 04

    Remove from the oven and add the chicken broth and coconut milk. Bring to a low simmer and cook for 10 minutes.

  5. 05

    Cool first, then purée with an immersion blender or a regular blender. Reheat.

  6. 06

    Serve with a small dollop of cream or Greek yogurt, a little coconut, some chopped cilantro, and a sprinkle of roughly chopped cashews.

From the printout

Written beside the curry paste. Only 1 full tsp. The printout says 1–4 teaspoons, and 2 is medium spicy.

In the margin. Apple and potato — don’t peel. Pumpkin purée: 1.875 cups, 30 tbsp. Coconut milk: a full 400 ml can, not the 14½-ounce can printed.

At the bottom. Cool first, then blend — reheat. Garnish coconut.
