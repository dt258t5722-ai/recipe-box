Butter Tarts

The Canadian classic: flaky pastry cups filled with a gooey brown-sugar and corn-syrup custard over plump raisins. The family note on the cookbook page stretches the filling to 18 tarts.

  • Spiral-bound cookbook, with family changes
  • Makes 12 medium tarts, or 18 regular muffin-size tarts
  • 375°F
  • 15–18 minutes

Ingredients

  • Pastry for 12 tart shells
  • ½ cup raisins
  • ¼ cup butter or margarine
  • ½ cup lightly packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup corn syrup
  • 2 eggs, slightly beaten
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 375°F.

  2. 02

    Roll the pastry out on a floured surface. Cut it with a floured cookie cutter and line 12 medium-sized muffin cups. Don't prick the pastry.

  3. 03

    Pour enough boiling water over the raisins to cover them. Let them stand until plump, then drain and arrange them in the tart shells.

  4. 04

    Mix together the butter and brown sugar. Stir in the corn syrup, eggs, salt and vanilla, and combine just until blended.

  5. 05

    Spoon into the unbaked tart shells, filling each about ⅔ full.

  6. 06

    Bake in the preheated 375°F oven for 15 to 18 minutes. Don't let the filling bubble over.

From the cookbook

Family changes. Handwritten in the cookbook: makes enough for 18 regular muffin-size tarts, using about 2 tablespoons of finely chopped raisins (or currants) per tart.
