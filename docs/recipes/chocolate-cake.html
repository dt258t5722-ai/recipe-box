Chocolate Cake

A two-layer cocoa cake, sifted straight into the mixer bowl and beaten in two short rounds — one minute, then two.

  • Cookbook page
  • Two 9-inch layers
  • 350°F
  • 35–40 minutes

Ingredients

Dry

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • ⅔ cup cocoa
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup granulated sugar

Wet

  • ⅔ cup shortening
  • 1 cup lightly packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup milk, plus ¼ cup
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 eggs

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease two 9-inch round layer cake pans and line the bottoms with wax paper.

  2. 02

    Sift the flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and granulated sugar together into a large mixer bowl.

  3. 03

    Add the shortening, brown sugar, 1 cup of the milk, and the vanilla. Beat for 1 minute at low speed of an electric mixer, or 150 strokes by hand.

  4. 04

    Add the remaining ¼ cup milk and the eggs. Beat for 2 minutes at low speed.

  5. 05

    Pour the batter into the prepared pans. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, or until the cake springs back when lightly touched.

  6. 06

    Cool 5 minutes, then remove from the pans.

From the cookbook page

From the page. No mixer? 150 strokes by hand does the same job.
