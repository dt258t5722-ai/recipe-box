Easy 4-Ingredient No-Knead French Bread

Just water, yeast, flour and salt. There's no kneading, only a couple of quick stretch-and-folds. You get three small loaves with a thin, crackly crust and a light, airy crumb. They're lovely with soup, or just with a little soft butter.

  • Food Language on YouTube
  • Makes 3 small loaves
  • 430°F (220°C)
  • About 4 hours, mostly rising time

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (220 ml) water
  • ½ tsp (2 g) dry yeast, or ¾ tsp (3 g) fresh yeast
  • 2½ cups (320 g) bread flour
  • 1 tsp (6 g) salt

Method

Mix

  1. 01

    Combine the water, yeast, flour and salt, and mix just until there's no dry flour left. There's no need to knead.

  2. 02

    Cover and let rest for 30 minutes.

Stretch, fold and rise

  1. 03

    With a wet hand, stretch each of the 4 sides of the dough up and fold it into the centre.

  2. 04

    Lift the dough and slap it back down a few times. This "lift and slap" only takes about 15 seconds, but it's important.

  3. 05

    Cover and let rest for another 30 minutes.

  4. 06

    Repeat the stretch and fold on all 4 sides. The dough will now be much more elastic. Give it another gentle lift and slap, about 12 seconds.

  5. 07

    Cover and let rise until doubled in size, usually about 2 hours.

Shape

  1. 08

    Sprinkle flour on the work surface and turn out the dough. Fold it over to make it easier to cut, then divide it into 3 equal pieces. Bubbles are a good sign.

  2. 09

    Brush off any excess flour. Flatten each piece gently, fold it into a tight ball, and rub it against the work surface to seal. (You can also flatten it, roll it up lengthwise, then fold it into a ball.)

  3. 10

    Let the balls rest uncovered for 10 minutes.

  4. 11

    Roll each ball into a longer loaf shape and move it to the baking tray.

  5. 12

    Cover with a towel and let rise for 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 430°F (220°C) while they rise.

Bake

  1. 13

    Lightly dust the loaves with flour (a tea strainer works well) and smooth it with your hand.

  2. 14

    With a sharp blade or knife, make three quick diagonal cuts in each loaf.

  3. 15

    Sprinkle a little water around the bread for steam, then cover with a second tray of the same size, turned upside down.

  4. 16

    Bake at 430°F (220°C) for 20 minutes. Remove the top tray and bake 10 minutes more, or until you're happy with the colour.

  5. 17

    Let cool. The crust crackles as it cools, and a hollow tap on the bottom means it's done.

Notes

Baking pan. The video bakes the loaves in a baguette tray covered with a second, upside-down tray to trap the steam. If you have only one tray, you can cover it with aluminum foil for the first 20 minutes instead.

Oven preheating. The video doesn't say exactly when to turn on the oven. Preheating it during the final 30-minute rise is my suggestion.
