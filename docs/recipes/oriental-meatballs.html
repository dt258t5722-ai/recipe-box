Oriental Meatballs

Gingersnap meatballs baked, then finished in a ketchup and pineapple sauce. The clipping says soy sauce twice. The margin says to add it carefully, in the sauce.

  • Newspaper clipping
  • 6 servings · 24 meatballs
  • 350°F
  • 10 minutes, then 10–15 more in the sauce

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 1½ pounds lean ground beef
  • 1 cup fine-crushed gingersnaps
  • 1 medium egg
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 (398 mL) can crushed pineapple, drained well, syrup reserved
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder

Sauce

  • ¾ cup ketchup
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • ½ teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1½ teaspoons dry mustard
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • ⅓ cup pineapple syrup
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Method

Meatballs

  1. 01

    Combine the beef, gingersnap crumbs, egg, soy sauce, crushed pineapple, salt, mustard, pepper, and garlic powder.

  2. 02

    Form into 24 meatballs, about 1½ inches across. Place in a shallow baking pan.

  3. 03

    Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.

Sauce

  1. 04

    Combine the ketchup, soy sauce, ginger, mustard, brown sugar, and pineapple syrup.

  2. 05

    Pour over the meatballs and continue baking 10 to 15 minutes longer.

  3. 06

    Serve over hot rice.

From the original card

Written beside the soy sauce. Add the soy sauce carefully, in the sauce portion.

In the margin. Add honey to the sauce. Garlic. Use cookie crumb — blend till very fine.
