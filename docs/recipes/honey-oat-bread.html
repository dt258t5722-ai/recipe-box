Soft & Sweet Honey Oat Bread

A soft, tender sandwich loaf sweetened with honey and studded with oats, finished with a honey glaze and a scattering of oats on top. A favourite homemade bread, perfect toasted.

  • Jessica & Nellie
  • Serves 12
  • 9 × 5-inch loaf pan
  • 350°F (180°C)
  • 40–50 minutes

Ingredients

Dough

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour (can use half whole wheat flour)
  • ¾ cup oats, instant or old-fashioned
  • 1 tbsp instant yeast
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • ¼ cup lukewarm water
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • ¼ cup honey

Topping

  • 1½ to 2 tablespoons honey, warmed
  • 1½ to 2 tablespoons oats

Method

Make the dough

  1. 01

    In a large bowl or stand mixer, combine the flour, oats, yeast and salt.

  2. 02

    Warm the milk until hot enough to melt the butter, but not boiling. Stir in the butter until melted, then the water and honey.

  3. 03

    Pour the milk mixture into the flour and mix with a dough hook until it comes together. Knead 7 to 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic. If still very sticky after 5 minutes, add flour 1 tablespoon at a time until barely tacky; if too dry, add water 1 teaspoon at a time.

  4. 04

    Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled, about ½ to 1 hour.

Shape and bake

  1. 05

    On a clean surface, flatten the dough into a 9 × 12-inch rectangle. Roll it up tightly, tucking the ends, and place in a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan.

  2. 06

    Brush the top with the warmed honey and sprinkle with oats. Let rise somewhere slightly warm until doubled, about ½ hour.

  3. 07

    Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and bake 40 to 50 minutes, until deep golden brown and cooked through.

Notes from the recipe

Warm spot. In winter, turn the oven on to 350° for a minute or two, then off, and let the loaf rise inside with the light on. You can leave it in while the oven preheats.
