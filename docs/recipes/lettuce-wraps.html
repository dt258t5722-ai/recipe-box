Lettuce Wraps

Minced chicken or pork, a hoisin-oyster sauce, and whole lettuce leaves washed the day before. Roll them at the table.

  • Handwritten card
  • Lettuce cups, to share
  • Lettuce washed and chilled the day before

Ingredients

Filling

  • Whole lettuce leaves
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 large green onions, minced
  • 1 whole red pepper, minced
  • A chunk of fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 carrot, minced
  • 1 small can water chestnuts, minced
  • 1 pound chicken or pork, minced
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • A handful of steamed noodles

Sauce

  • 3 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  • ½ cup oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sugar

Method

  1. 01

    Prepare all the ingredients and the sauce ahead of cooking.

  2. 02

    Have whole lettuce leaves washed and chilled the day before. When washing, separate each leaf individually.

  3. 03

    Mince the garlic, green onions, red pepper, ginger, carrot, and water chestnuts.

  4. 04

    Make the sauce by combining the hoisin, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and sugar in a bowl. Stir to mix.

  5. 05

    Heat olive oil in a fry pan over medium heat. Gently cook the garlic, onions, red pepper, ginger, carrot, and water chestnuts.

  6. 06

    Add 1 pound of minced chicken or pork. Stir to break it up and cook until cooked through. Season with salt and pepper. Add the sauce to the meat mixture. Just before serving, add a handful of steamed noodles.

  7. 07

    Spoon into lettuce cups. Drizzle with hoisin. Eat and roll up.

From the original card

The day before. Wash and chill the leaves. Separate each one.

In the margin. Hoisin is 3 tablespoons, with “¾ cup + 1 Tbsp” written beside it.
