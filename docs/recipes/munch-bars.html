Camp Squeah Munch Bars

Oats, Rice Krispies, coconut, and sunflower seeds, bound with melted marshmallows and finished with chocolate chips. A camp recipe, made by the potful.

  • Camp Squeah newsletter
  • A big batch
  • No-bake

Ingredients

Cereal mixture

  • 4 cups rolled oats
  • 1½ cups Rice Krispies
  • 1½ cups shredded coconut
  • 2½ cups sunflower seeds

To bind

  • ⅔ cup margarine
  • 15 cups marshmallows

To finish

  • 1¼ cups chocolate chips

Method

  1. 01

    Combine the rolled oats, Rice Krispies, coconut, and sunflower seeds in a large bowl.

  2. 02

    In a large pot, melt the margarine. Add the marshmallows and stir.

  3. 03

    Mix in the cereal mixture.

  4. 04

    Add the chocolate chips.

From the newsletter

Good to know. The newsletter stops at the chocolate chips. It gives no pan size or setting time.
