Jiffy Cinnamon Buns

Biscuit dough, brown-sugar butter, baked in muffin tins. Flip them out the moment they come from the oven.

  • Canadian Living, May 1989
  • 12 buns
  • 400°F
  • 15–20 minutes — watch them

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup cold butter
  • 1 cup milk (reduce just a bit)

Topping

  • ⅓ cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 Tbsp cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup raisins

Method

  1. 01

    Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Blend in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse meal.

  2. 02

    Pour in the milk, stirring lightly. Turn onto a floured surface and knead 8–10 times. Roll into a 12 × 8-inch rectangle.

  3. 03

    Cream the butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Spread over the dough. Sprinkle with raisins.

  4. 04

    Roll up jelly-roll style from the long side, seam under. Cut into 12 pieces. Place in greased muffin tins.

  5. 05

    Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes. Watch the time — check after 15 minutes. Flip out immediately.

From the original card

From the card. Roll out like a log, then seam under.

The usual way. I usually take them out after 15 minutes.
