Super Salmon Loaf

The Recipe Box / Supper

Super Salmon Loaf

A mid-century loaf, baked in a water bath and turned out onto a warm platter. The clipping called for parsley. The card says dill.

  • Newspaper clipping
  • 6 servings
  • 350°F
  • 45–55 minutes, in a water bath

Ingredients

  • 2 (7¾-ounce) cans salmon
  • 4 tablespoons butter or margarine
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 cup unsalted cracker crumbs
  • 3 medium eggs, separated
  • 2 tablespoons chopped dill (the clipping said parsley)
  • ¼ cup finely chopped onion
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon pepper

Method

  1. 01

    Drain the salmon juices into a medium saucepan. Add the butter and milk to the salmon juice and heat just until the butter melts. Remove from heat; stir in the cracker crumbs and let stand five minutes.

  2. 02

    Flake the salmon and fold into the crumb mixture along with the well-mashed bones. Beat the egg yolks and fold into the salmon mixture along with the remaining ingredients except the egg whites. Beat the egg whites until they form soft peaks. Fold into the salmon mixture.

  3. 03

    Grease an 8½ × 4½-inch loaf pan and line with wax paper, leaving about an inch of paper to form a collar around the edges of the pan. Grease the paper. Pour the salmon mixture into the prepared pan and set in a shallow baking pan. Pour boiling water into the baking pan to a depth of one inch.

  4. 04

    Bake in a moderate oven, 350°F, for 45 to 55 minutes. Remove the pan from the water. Let stand for five minutes before unmoulding onto a heated platter. Garnish with lemon slices.

From the original card

Written in the margin. Dill — not parsley.
