Roberta's Pizza Dough

The famous Brooklyn pizzeria's dough: a mix of silky 00 and chewy bread flour, just a pinch of yeast, and a slow rise. It's ready in a few hours, but even better after a day in the fridge.

  • Pizza Dough Showdown chart
  • Makes 2 pizza crusts
  • Rise 3–4 hours, or 18–24 hours

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1 cup 00 flour
  • 1 cup bread flour
  • 1 cup warm tap water
  • 1 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • ½ teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil

Method

  1. 01

    Stir the yeast into the warm water and let it sit for a few minutes.

  2. 02

    Whisk the two flours and the salt together in a large bowl.

  3. 03

    Add the yeast water and olive oil and mix until a shaggy dough forms.

  4. 04

    Knead on a lightly floured surface until smooth and elastic, about 3 minutes.

  5. 05

    Let rest 15 minutes, then knead briefly again.

  6. 06

    Divide into 2 balls, cover, and let rise 3 to 4 hours at room temperature, or 18 to 24 hours in the fridge.

  7. 07

    Stretch each ball into a round, top as you like, and bake in a very hot oven.

Notes

From the chart. The chart gives only the ingredients, the rising time and the yield. The steps above are the usual method for this dough.

Slow rise. The 18–24 hour fridge rise gives the most flavour. Let the dough come to room temperature before you stretch it.
