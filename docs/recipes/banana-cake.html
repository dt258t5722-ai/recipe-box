Banana Cake

Ripe bananas and buttermilk, added alternately with the flour. Two 8-inch layers, from page 124.

  • Cookbook page, p. 124
  • Two 8-inch layers
  • 350°F
  • 30–35 minutes

Ingredients

Dry

  • 2¼ cups all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt

Creamed

  • ½ cup shortening
  • 1¼ cups granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Liquid

  • 1 cup mashed ripe bananas
  • ¾ cup buttermilk

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease two 8-inch round layer cake pans and line the bottoms with wax paper.

  2. 02

    Sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.

  3. 03

    Cream the shortening. Beat in the sugar, then blend in the eggs and vanilla, beating until light and fluffy.

  4. 04

    Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture alternately with the bananas and buttermilk — 3 dry and 2 liquid additions, combining lightly after each.

  5. 05

    Turn into the prepared pans. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the cake springs back when lightly touched.

  6. 06

    Cool 5 minutes, then remove from the pans.
