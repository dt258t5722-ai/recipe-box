White Chocolate Cheesecake Truffles

No-bake bites of buttery biscuit cheesecake, dipped in white chocolate and finished with a pretty drizzle. They take just four ingredients and an hour in the freezer.

  • From a video
  • No bake
  • Freeze 30–60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 80 g crunchy biscuits (shortcake works well)
  • Cream cheese
  • 50 g icing sugar
  • 150 g white chocolate, melted, plus a little extra for drizzling

Method

  1. 01

    Crush the biscuits in a food processor, or in a bag with a rolling pin, and tip them into a bowl.

  2. 02

    Add the cream cheese and icing sugar and mix with a spoon until it comes together into a thick paste.

  3. 03

    Scoop up 1 tablespoon at a time and roll into smooth balls between your hands. Set them on a plate.

  4. 04

    Freeze for 30 to 60 minutes, until firm.

  5. 05

    Using two forks, dip each ball into the melted white chocolate to coat it, letting the extra drip off. Set on a wire rack.

  6. 06

    Drizzle more melted white chocolate back and forth over the tops.

From the video

About the cream cheese. The video doesn't say how much cream cheese to use. Add enough to bind the crumbs into a thick paste that holds its shape when rolled.
