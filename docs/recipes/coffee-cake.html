Coffee Cake

A tender vanilla cake with a brown-sugar cinnamon crumb and chopped walnuts on top. It's the sort of square you cut while it's still a little warm and eat with a cup of coffee.

  • Cookbook, page 202
  • One 9-inch square pan
  • 350°F
  • 45–50 minutes

Ingredients

Cake

  • ⅓ cup shortening
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk

Topping

  • ½ cup lightly packed brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter or margarine

Method

Make the cake

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch square cake pan.

  2. 02

    Cream the shortening (⅓ cup) and granulated sugar (1 cup).

  3. 03

    Beat in the egg (1) and vanilla (1 teaspoon) until light and fluffy.

  4. 04

    Sift together the flour (1½ cups), baking powder (2 teaspoons) and salt (½ teaspoon).

  5. 05

    Add the dry ingredients alternately with the milk (1 cup), in 3 dry and 2 liquid additions, mixing lightly after each.

  6. 06

    Turn the batter into the prepared pan.

Top and bake

  1. 07

    Mix the brown sugar (½ cup, lightly packed), flour (2 tablespoons), cinnamon (1 teaspoon), chopped walnuts (½ cup) and melted butter or margarine (3 tablespoons).

  2. 08

    Sprinkle the topping over the batter.

  3. 09

    Bake at 350°F for 45–50 minutes, until golden brown.

Notes
