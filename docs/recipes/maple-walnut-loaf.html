Maple Walnut Loaf with Maple Glaze

A tender quick loaf sweetened with maple syrup and applesauce, warmed with a touch of nutmeg and full of chopped walnuts. A drizzle of maple glaze on top adds even more maple flavour.

  • Little Frugal Homestead
  • Serves 10
  • One loaf pan
  • 350°F
  • 1 hour

Ingredients

Dry ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 3 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ teaspoon nutmeg

Wet ingredients

  • 1 stick (½ cup) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 2 eggs, at room temperature
  • ¾ cup maple syrup, at room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Additions

  • 1 cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 1 cup walnuts, chopped

Maple glaze

  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup, at room temperature
  • ½ tablespoon milk or water

Method

The loaf

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F and prepare a loaf pan with baking spray or a butter wrapper.

  2. 02

    In a small bowl, combine the dry ingredients and set aside.

  3. 03

    In a separate, larger bowl, combine the cooled melted butter with the maple syrup. Mix well.

  4. 04

    Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each. Stir in the vanilla, then the applesauce.

  5. 05

    Add the dry ingredients a few scoops at a time, stirring well after each addition. Stir in the walnuts.

  6. 06

    Pour into the prepared pan and bake at 350°F for 1 hour, until a toothpick or cake tester comes out clean.

  7. 07

    Cool in the pan on a wire rack for 20 minutes, then turn out and let it cool completely on the rack.

Maple glaze

  1. 08

    In a small bowl, combine the powdered sugar, maple syrup and milk until pourable. Add more milk or sugar as needed to get the consistency you like.

  2. 09

    Drizzle over the cooled loaf.
