Lemon Coconut Cake

Written out in green pencil, with a drawing at the top. Coconut milk and coconut oil, brightened with lemon.

  • Handwritten card
  • 350°F
  • 60 minutes

Ingredients

Dry

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Lemon zest (the card just says “lemon”)

Wet

  • ¾ cup coconut milk
  • 2 eggs
  • Lemon juice
  • ½ cup coconut oil

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a cake pan.

  2. 02

    Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and lemon zest in a bowl.

  3. 03

    In another bowl, whisk the eggs with the coconut milk, coconut oil, and lemon juice.

  4. 04

    Stir the wet ingredients into the dry until just combined, and pour into the pan.

  5. 05

    Bake at 350° for 60 minutes.

From the original card

On the card. 350° — 60 minutes.

Good to know. The card lists ingredients only, with no method or pan size. The steps here simply put them together.
