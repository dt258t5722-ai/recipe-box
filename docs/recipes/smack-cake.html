Smack Cake

A crumbly butter base and a coconut meringue top. The card is very clear about the coconut: stir, do not beat.

  • Typed card
  • One 8 × 8 pan
  • 350°F
  • 25–30 minutes

Ingredients

Base

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • ½ cup melted butter
  • 2 egg yolks, beaten slightly
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Topping

  • 2 egg whites
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup medium unsweetened coconut

Method

Base

  1. 01

    Set the oven at 350°F.

  2. 02

    Lightly grease an 8 × 8 baking pan, or line it with foil for easy removal.

  3. 03

    Mix the dry ingredients.

  4. 04

    Mix together the egg yolks and vanilla.

  5. 05

    Combine the dry ingredients, egg, and melted butter to form a crumbly mixture.

  6. 06

    Pat the mixture fairly firmly into the prepared pan.

Topping

  1. 07

    Beat the egg whites until they are stiff.

  2. 08

    Add the salt and sugar and beat again.

  3. 09

    Stir in (very important to stir, not beat) the coconut.

  4. 10

    Gently spread the topping onto the base layer.

  5. 11

    Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes.

  6. 12

    Cool on a rack.

From the original card

Very important. Stir in the coconut — do not beat.
