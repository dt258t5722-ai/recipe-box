Bread Machine Recipes

Every loaf from the bread maker's booklet in one place: white, quick, French, ultra fast, whole wheat, a cake, and a dough setting for rolls and pizza. Pick a recipe and size, load the pan in order, and let the machine do the rest.

  • Bread maker booklet
  • 700 g or 900 g loaves
  • Times are the machine's cooking time

Ingredients

White Bread, 700 g (2:53)

  • 270 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 3 cups bread flour
  • 1 tsp yeast

White Bread, 900 g (3:00)

  • 330 ml water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 tsp yeast

Quick, 700 g (2:35)

  • 250 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1½ tbsp sugar
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp yeast

French Bread, 700 g (3:40)

  • 250 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1½ tbsp sugar
  • 3 cups bread flour
  • 1 tsp yeast

French Bread, 900 g (3:50), grows too large

  • 330 ml water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 tsp yeast

Ultra Fast Bread, 900 g (2:05)

  • 300 ml water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 3½ cups bread flour
  • 2 tsp yeast

Whole Wheat Bread, 700 g (3:37)

  • 260 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 2 cups bread flour
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk powder
  • 1 tsp yeast

Whole Wheat Bread, 900 g (3:45)

  • 330 ml water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 2 cups bread flour
  • 2½ tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk powder
  • 1 tsp yeast

Cake, 700 g (1:50)

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 8 tbsp sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 1⅔ cups self-rising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp yeast

Dough, 700 g (1:30)

  • 360 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 tsp yeast

Method

  1. 01

    Always add ingredients in this order: liquids first, then dry ingredients, then the yeast (or baking soda/powder) last. Yeast should never touch the liquid or the salt.

  2. 02

    For recipes with eggs, mix the eggs in with the liquid ingredients.

  3. 03

    Choose the program and the bread size to match the recipe. Bread size is the size of the finished loaf.

  4. 04

    Start the machine. The time beside each recipe is how long the program runs.

  5. 05

    On the Dough setting the machine only kneads and rises. Shape the dough by hand into rolls, pizza or specialty breads and bake them in a regular oven.

From the booklet

Measuring. The cup and spoon that came with the machine aren't standard sizes. Its cup holds about 200 ml of liquid or 140 g of flour, and its spoon holds about 1 tsp (7 g) or 1 tbsp (12 g). These recipes were tested with them.

From the family. The 900 g French bread grows too large, so stick with the 700 g size.
