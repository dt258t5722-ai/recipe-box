Jelly Roll

A quick sponge baked thin, turned out onto a sugared towel and rolled while warm. Then fill it with jam, jelly, or filling.

  • Cookbook page
  • One 15 × 10 × 1-inch pan
  • 400°F
  • 10–12 minutes

Ingredients

Dry

  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt

Eggs

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ¼ cup hot milk

Method

Sponge

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 400°F. Grease a 15 × 10 × 1-inch jelly roll pan and line it with wax paper.

  2. 02

    Sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt.

  3. 03

    Beat the eggs, sugar, and lemon juice until thick and lemon-coloured. Gradually beat in the hot milk, and keep beating until very thick.

  4. 04

    Sift the dry ingredients over the egg mixture in four portions, folding gently after each.

  5. 05

    Turn into the prepared pan. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the cake springs back when lightly touched.

Rolling

  1. 06

    Turn out immediately onto a towel sprinkled with icing sugar.

  2. 07

    Peel off the wax paper and roll the cake up, rolling the towel up inside it. Let it cool.

  3. 08

    Unroll, fill with jam, jelly, or filling, and re-roll.

From the cookbook page

Don't wait. Turn it out the moment it leaves the oven, and roll it up in the towel while warm.
