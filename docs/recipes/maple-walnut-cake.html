Maple-Walnut Cake with Brown-Sugar Frosting

Two tender layers sweetened with pure maple syrup and studded with toasted walnuts, covered in a silky brown-sugar cream cheese frosting and crowned with walnut halves. A showpiece for autumn birthdays and Sunday dinners.

  • Magazine clipping, with family changes
  • Serves 10
  • Two 9-inch round pans
  • 350°F
  • 30–35 minutes

Ingredients

For the cake

  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour (spooned and leveled), plus more for pans
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • ¾ teaspoon fine salt
  • ¾ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • ½ cup packed dark-brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1½ cups buttermilk
  • 1 cup chopped walnut halves, toasted

For the frosting

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
  • ¾ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • ¾ cup packed dark-brown sugar
  • 2 cups confectioners' sugar

To decorate

  • Walnut halves

Method

Make the cake

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Coat two 9-inch round cake pans (2 inches deep) with cooking spray, line the bottoms with parchment, spray the parchment, then flour the pans, tapping out the excess.

  2. 02

    Whisk together the flour, baking powder and salt.

  3. 03

    In a large bowl, beat the butter and brown sugar on high until light and fluffy, 5 minutes. Beat in the eggs one at a time, scraping down the bowl.

  4. 04

    With the mixer on medium-high, add the maple syrup in a slow, steady stream.

  5. 05

    Add the flour mixture in two additions, alternating with the buttermilk, beating well after each. Fold in the walnuts.

  6. 06

    Divide between the pans and tap them firmly on the counter a few times to knock out air bubbles. Bake 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick in the centre comes out clean.

  7. 07

    Cool in the pans 15 minutes, then turn out onto racks, peel off the parchment, flip right side up and cool completely. Trim the domed tops flat with a serrated knife.

Frost and assemble

  1. 08

    Beat the cream cheese, butter and brown sugar on high until light and fluffy, 5 minutes. Add the confectioners' sugar ½ cup at a time, beating well after each.

  2. 09

    Set one layer top side up on a cake stand and spread with 1½ cups frosting.

  3. 10

    Top with the second layer, top side down, and spread the rest of the frosting over the top and sides.

  4. 11

    Ring the top with walnut halves.

From the clipping

Family changes. Handwritten on the clipping: 3 cups flour, 1 cup maple syrup (instead of 1½ cups), and 1½ cups buttermilk in place of ¾ cup whole milk.

Make ahead. Refrigerate the frosted cake in an airtight container up to 1 day. Let it sit at room temperature 1 hour before serving.
