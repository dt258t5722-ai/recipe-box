Jo-Ann's Easy Shepherd's Pie

Savoury herbed pork in mushroom sauce, layered with tender spinach and creamed corn under a blanket of garlicky mashed red potatoes. It bakes until the top is golden and the edges are crisp.

  • Jo-Ann's recipe
  • Long rectangular baking dish
  • 375°F
  • About 45 minutes

Ingredients

Potatoes

  • 6 large (or 8 medium) red-skinned potatoes
  • A few garlic cloves (optional, for extra flavour)
  • Butter, salt and pepper to taste

Filling

  • 1 package fresh spinach (or thawed frozen spinach, squeezed dry)
  • 1 large onion (or 2 medium), chopped small
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped small
  • Olive oil
  • 1 package ground pork
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 tablespoons dried herb, any you like (basil, marjoram)
  • 1 can Campbell's mushroom soup
  • Grated carrot or diced leftover veggies (optional)
  • 1 can Green Giant creamed corn

To serve

  • Ketchup

Method

Spinach and potatoes

  1. 01

    Put the spinach in a large pot with just 1 to 2 tablespoons of water. Cover and steam until wilted, 3 to 5 minutes, checking often and moving the leaves so they don't burn. Drain, press gently to remove extra moisture, and set aside on a plate.

  2. 02

    In the same pot, boil or steam the potatoes, adding a few garlic cloves for extra flavour. When fully cooked (test with a knife), drain and mash with the garlic, working in dollops of butter and salt and pepper to taste.

While the potatoes cook

  1. 03

    Fry the onion and garlic in olive oil until softened, then set aside on a plate.

  2. 04

    Add more oil to the pan and stir-fry the ground pork until no longer pink. Season with salt and pepper. Add back the onion and garlic with the dried herb and the mushroom soup, and stir to combine.

  3. 05

    For more veggies, mix in some grated carrot or diced leftover vegetables.

Assemble and bake

  1. 06

    Oil a long rectangular baking dish and spread the meat over the bottom.

  2. 07

    Layer on the spinach, then spread the creamed corn over the spinach.

  3. 08

    Top it all with a full layer of mashed potatoes, using a fork to spread them and make a pattern of lines on top.

  4. 09

    Set the dish on a foil-lined baking tray to catch any drips.

  5. 10

    Bake at 375°F for about 45 minutes, until the potato topping is nicely browned and the edges are a bit crispy. Serve with ketchup.

From the recipe

About the pork. The recipe calls for "1 package" of ground pork without giving a weight.
