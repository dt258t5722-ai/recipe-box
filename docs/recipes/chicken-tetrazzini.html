Chicken Tetrazzini

Spaghetti and chicken in a creamy three-part sauce, with mushrooms, pimientos, and Parmesan. One batch fills two dishes: one for tonight, one for the freezer or a friend.

  • Taste of Home
  • 2 casseroles, 4 servings each
  • Two 11 × 7-inch dishes
  • 350°F
  • 20–25 minutes

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 1 package (12 ounces) spaghetti

Sauce

  • ⅓ cup butter, cubed
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • 1 can (14½ ounces) chicken broth
  • 1½ cups half-and-half cream
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream

Filling

  • 4 cups cubed cooked chicken
  • 3 cans (4 ounces each) mushroom stems and pieces, drained
  • 1 jar (4 ounces) sliced pimientos, drained

Topping

  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Method

Sauce and pasta

  1. 01

    Cook the spaghetti until al dente, according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.

  2. 02

    In a Dutch oven, melt the butter. Stir in the flour, salt, and pepper until smooth. Gradually whisk in the broth, half-and-half, and whipping cream.

  3. 03

    Bring to a boil, then cook and stir until thickened, about 2 minutes.

  4. 04

    Remove from the heat. Stir in the chicken, mushrooms, pimientos, and spaghetti, tossing gently to coat.

Bake one

  1. 05

    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Divide the mixture between two greased 11 × 7-inch baking dishes and sprinkle with Parmesan.

  2. 06

    Bake one casserole, uncovered, until heated through, 20 to 25 minutes.

Freeze one

  1. 07

    Cover and freeze the second casserole for up to 2 months.

  2. 08

    To serve, thaw in the refrigerator overnight. Cover and bake at 350° for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake until heated through, 15 to 20 minutes more. Stir before serving.

Notes from the recipe

From the recipe. Cook the spaghetti only to al dente. It soaks up sauce in the oven without going mushy.

For the freezer. Up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then bake covered first.

Before serving. Give the reheated casserole a stir.
