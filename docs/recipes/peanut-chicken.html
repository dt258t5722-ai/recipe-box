Peanut Chicken Stir Fry

Broiled chicken, a pan of steamed vegetables, rice cooked in broth — and the peanut-hoisin sauce served on the side, to drizzle.

  • Handwritten binder page
  • A large platter, family-style
  • Marinate 10 minutes; grill about 12 minutes

Ingredients

Chicken & rice

  • Chicken breasts, sliced into thin strips
  • A little olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Rice, cooked in chicken broth

Sauce

  • 1 cup chicken broth (or water)
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • ⅔ cup hoisin sauce
  • 4 Tbsp cider vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Water, to thin if the sauce becomes too thick

Vegetables

  • Broccoli, thinly sliced
  • Celery, thinly sliced
  • Carrots, thinly sliced
  • Red pepper, thinly sliced

Method

  1. 01

    Slice the chicken breasts into thin strips. Place in a bowl and toss with a little olive oil for at least 10 minutes. Place the chicken on a foil-lined sheet and season with salt and pepper.

  2. 02

    Wash and cook the rice in chicken broth.

  3. 03

    In a pot, mix the sauce — broth, peanut butter, hoisin, cider vinegar, and garlic — and simmer. Thin with water if it becomes too thick.

  4. 04

    Turn the broiler to high and move the rack to the second rung.

  5. 05

    Wash and thinly slice the broccoli, celery, carrots, and red pepper.

  6. 06

    Grill the chicken 6 minutes on one side. Flip and grill 6 minutes on the second side.

  7. 07

    Cook the red peppers in hot oil in an electric fry pan. Then add the broccoli, carrots, and celery. Put on the lid. Add a little, little water (not much) to steam.

  8. 08

    Put the rice on a large platter. Top with the vegetables and chicken.

  9. 09

    Put the sauce in a separate bowl to drizzle over the vegetables and chicken.

From the original card

From the card. A little, little water — not much — to steam. Sauce stays on the side, for drizzling.
