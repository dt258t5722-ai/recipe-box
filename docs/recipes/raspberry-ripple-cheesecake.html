Raspberry Ripple Cheesecake

No oven needed: a buttery graham cracker base, a whipped cream-cheese filling, and a homemade raspberry purée swirled and feathered on top.

  • Children's cookbook page
  • Serves 8
  • One 8-inch (20 cm) removable-base pan
  • No-bake
  • Chill 2 hours or overnight

Ingredients

Base

  • 1 stick (8 tbsp) butter
  • 8 oz (250 g) graham crackers

Raspberry purée

  • 2½ cups fresh raspberries
  • ⅔ cup powdered sugar

Filling

  • 16 oz (450 g) cream cheese
  • 1 cup superfine sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1⅔ cups whipping cream

Method

Base

  1. 01

    Put the crackers in a bag and roll with a rolling pin to make crumbs.

  2. 02

    Melt the butter in a pan. Pour in the crushed graham crackers and stir thoroughly.

  3. 03

    Line an 8-inch (20 cm) cake pan with a removable base with plastic wrap. Spoon in the cracker mixture, press flat with a masher, and refrigerate.

Purée

  1. 04

    Bring the raspberries and powdered sugar to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes. Cool, then press through a sieve.

Filling

  1. 05

    Mix together the cream cheese, superfine sugar, and vanilla. Whip the cream until stiff, then fold it into the cheese mixture.

  2. 06

    Spread about ¾ of the cheese mixture over the cracker base. Spoon on ¾ of the purée and swirl it into the cheesecake mixture.

  3. 07

    Gently spread on the remaining cheesecake mixture. Drizzle straight lines of the remaining purée across the top and pull a skewer across the lines for a feathered effect.

  4. 08

    Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
