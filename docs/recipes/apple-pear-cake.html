Apple & Pear Cake with Lemon Cream

Tender layers of apple and a soft pear-studded batter, crowned with a silky lemon custard and toasted almonds that bake right on top. It's moist, generous, and perfect with a cup of coffee.

  • From a YouTube baking video
  • 180°C (350°F)
  • 50–55 minutes

Ingredients

Lemon cream

  • 1 egg
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 4 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 240 ml milk (about 1 cup)
  • 50 g butter (about 3½ tablespoons)

Cake

  • 4 eggs
  • 120 g sugar (about ½ cup + 1 tablespoon)
  • 90 g Greek yogurt or sour cream (about ⅓ cup)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla sugar
  • 5–6 g salt (about 1 teaspoon)
  • 80 ml vegetable oil (about ⅓ cup)
  • 270 g flour (about 2¼ cups), sifted
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 pears, cut into pieces

Layers and topping

  • Apples, peeled and cut into pieces (amount not given)
  • Almond flakes, lightly toasted (amount not given)
  • Vegetable oil for the pan
  • Powdered sugar for dusting

Method

Make the lemon cream

  1. 01

    Mix the egg, lemon zest, sugar and cornstarch well.

  2. 02

    Stir in the milk. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, and cook until the cream thickens.

  3. 03

    Stir in the butter. Transfer to a bowl, cover the surface with plastic wrap, and let cool.

Make the batter

  1. 04

    Beat the eggs with the sugar, then mix in the yogurt or sour cream, vanilla sugar, salt and oil.

  2. 05

    Sift in the flour with the baking powder and mix until smooth.

  3. 06

    Fold in the pear pieces and mix everything well.

Layer and bake

  1. 07

    Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease the pan with vegetable oil.

  2. 08

    Spread some of the apple pieces over the bottom of the pan, then spread a thin layer of batter over them. Repeat two more times.

  3. 09

    Sprinkle the top with the toasted almond flakes.

  4. 10

    Put the cooled lemon cream in a piping bag and pipe or spread it over the cake however you like.

  5. 11

    Bake for 50–55 minutes.

  6. 12

    Let cool, then dust with powdered sugar.

Notes

What the video leaves out. The video doesn't give the number of apples, the amount of almond flakes, or the pan size. About 3 medium apples, a small handful of almonds, and a 9-inch (23 cm) springform pan would be sensible guesses, but they're mine, not the video's.

Order of the batter. The video shows the cake ingredients going in one by one but doesn't say exactly how to combine them. The mixing order here is the usual one.
