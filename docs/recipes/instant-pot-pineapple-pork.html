Instant Pot Pineapple Pork

Tender pork, juicy pineapple and sweet red pepper in a glossy honey, soy and ginger sauce. Brown everything right in the Instant Pot and dinner is ready after just ten minutes under pressure.

  • Mommy's Home Cooking
  • Instant Pot
  • 10 minutes at pressure, 5 minutes natural release

Ingredients

Sauce

  • Juice from the pineapple can
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon freshly grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced

Pork

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 pounds (900 g) boneless pork stew meat
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 20 ounces (570 g) pineapple chunks in pineapple juice, drained
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

To serve

  • Parsley, for garnish

Method

  1. 01

    Drain the pineapple, saving the juice. In a small bowl, mix the juice with the garlic, ginger, soy sauce, honey and brown sugar.

  2. 02

    Set the Instant Pot to Sauté on high. When hot, add 1 tablespoon oil and brown the pork in batches until no longer pink. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Add the remaining oil and soften the onion and red pepper for a few minutes.

  4. 04

    Return the pork, add the pineapple chunks and oregano, and season with salt and pepper. Stir to combine.

  5. 05

    Pour in the sauce, secure the lid and cook at pressure for 10 minutes.

  6. 06

    Let the pressure release on its own for 5 minutes, then carefully turn the valve to Venting to release the rest.

  7. 07

    Remove the lid. Stir the cornstarch into the water, add it, and sauté for a minute or two until the sauce thickens. Garnish with parsley and serve.

Notes

Filling the gaps. The original steps don't say when the onion, red pepper, brown sugar, cornstarch and water go in. They've been added where they usually go: the vegetables after browning, the sugar in the sauce, and the cornstarch at the end to thicken.

To serve. Lovely over steamed rice.
