Earl Grey Scones

Tender, golden wedges with the gentle bergamot of Earl Grey steeped right into the milk. Lovely warm from the oven with butter and a spoonful of apricot jam.

  • Twinings recipe card
  • Serves 8
  • 400°F (200°C)
  • 15 minutes

Ingredients

Tea-infused milk

  • 1 cup (250 mL) milk
  • 3 Twinings Earl Grey tea bags

Dough

  • 3 cups (750 mL) all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup (125 mL) white sugar
  • 5 tsp (25 mL) baking powder
  • ½ tsp (2 mL) salt
  • ¾ cup (180 mL) butter
  • 1 egg, beaten

Method

Steep the tea

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and lightly grease a baking sheet.

  2. 02

    Bring the milk to a simmer in a small pot, then take it off the heat. Add the tea bags and let them steep for 30 minutes before lifting them out.

Make the dough

  1. 03

    In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Cut in the butter.

  2. 04

    In a small bowl, mix the beaten egg with the tea-infused milk, then stir it into the flour mixture.

Shape and bake

  1. 05

    Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead briefly.

  2. 06

    Roll it into a ½-inch (1.3 cm) thick round and cut into 8 wedges.

  3. 07

    Place on the prepared sheet and bake for 15 minutes, until golden brown.

From the original card

Give it time. Let the tea steep the full 30 minutes; that's where all the flavour comes from.

To serve. Warm, with apricot jam, as pictured on the card.
