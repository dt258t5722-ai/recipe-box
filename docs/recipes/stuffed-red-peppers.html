Red Peppers Stuffed with Meat and Rice

Red pepper halves piled high with beef, pork, rice and Parmigiano. They bake on a garlicky sesame-tomato sauce and are finished with a golden layer of cheese and a dollop of sour cream.

  • Family recipe, with handwritten changes
  • 9 × 13-inch baking dish
  • 375°F
  • About 1 hour 10 minutes

Ingredients

Peppers and toppings

  • 4 large red peppers
  • Cooked rice (about 2 cups)
  • 2 cups grated cheese, preferably Parmigiano-Reggiano (1 cup for stuffing, 1 cup for topping)
  • 1 tub sour cream

Sauce

  • 1 onion, diced
  • 6–8 garlic cloves, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil (must use sesame seed oil)
  • 1 jar bruschetta, or Costco basil sauce
  • Fresh tomatoes processed into 1–2 cups purée, or bottled tomato sauce (such as Molisana strained tomatoes)
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper

Stuffing

  • 1 lb ground lean beef and ¼ lb ground Italian pork sausage (casings removed), or a ground beef and pork mix
  • Costco basil sauce (in place of the original guacamole)
  • Italian seasoning, for extra flavour
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. 01

    Preheat the oven to 375°F.

  2. 02

    Finely process the garlic in a food processor and put it in a frying pan with the sesame oil. Process the onion and add it to the pan. Gently cook the garlic and onion for 5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Put half the onion-garlic mix in a bowl and set aside.

  4. 04

    To the pan, stir in the bruschetta (or basil sauce), tomato purée, beef broth and balsamic vinegar. Simmer gently until thickened. Taste the sauce and add salt and pepper as needed.

  5. 05

    Pour the sauce into a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.

  6. 06

    To the reserved onion-garlic mix, add the meat, basil sauce, cooked rice, 1 cup of the cheese, and salt and pepper.

  7. 07

    Wash and dry the peppers, cut them in half lengthwise and scoop out the seeds.

  8. 08

    Over-stuff the peppers with the meat mixture so it mounds up nicely, and set them on the sauce in the dish.

  9. 09

    Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes.

  10. 10

    Remove the foil, sprinkle with the remaining cheese and bake another 20 to 25 minutes, until nicely browned.

  11. 11

    Serve the peppers with the sauce and top with dollops of sour cream.

From the recipe

Family changes. Handwritten on the recipe: must use sesame seed oil; Costco basil sauce works in place of the bruschetta; bottled tomatoes like Molisana strained tomatoes; a ground beef and pork mix can replace the beef and sausage; basil sauce replaces the guacamole or avocado; and the sauce simmers until thickened rather than for 1 minute.

Basil sauce amount. The changes don't say how much basil sauce goes into the stuffing. The original called for 1 small tub of guacamole, so use about that much.
