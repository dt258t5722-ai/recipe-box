Butterscotch Oat Thins

Thin, crisp squares of buttery brown-sugar oats with a sparkle of sugar on top. They need just a handful of pantry staples and a low, slow oven. This one is starred on the family clipping.

  • Magazine clipping, with family changes
  • 13 × 9½ × 2-inch pan
  • 300°F
  • 14–15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla
  • Sugar, for sprinkling

Method

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 300°F.

  2. 02

    Combine the rolled oats, baking powder, salt and brown sugar, blending well with a fork. Stir in the melted butter and vanilla.

  3. 03

    Spread in a thin layer in an ungreased 13 × 9½ × 2-inch baking pan. Press down firmly with the back of a spoon, then sprinkle generously with sugar.

  4. 04

    Bake until golden, 14 to 15 minutes. Let stand 5 minutes, then cut into squares or bars. Let them cool before taking them out of the pan.

From the clipping

Family changes. Handwritten on the clipping: bake 14 to 15 minutes instead of the printed "about 20 minutes". The recipe is also starred as a favourite.
