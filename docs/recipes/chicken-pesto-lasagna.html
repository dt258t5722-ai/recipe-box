Chicken Pesto Lasagna

A creamy white lasagna that skips the red sauce. Ground chicken, a garlicky ricotta and pesto filling, and roasted garlic parmesan sauce are layered three times under a golden, herb-flecked blanket of mozzarella.

  • From a recipe video
  • Square baking dish
  • 375°F
  • 25 minutes

Ingredients

Filling

  • Ground chicken, cooked
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Shredded mozzarella
  • Parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • Pesto (nut-free works)
  • 2 eggs

To assemble

  • 1 jar store-bought roasted garlic and parmesan sauce (or your own white sauce)
  • No-boil lasagna noodles
  • Plenty of extra shredded mozzarella
  • Italian seasoning

Method

Make the filling

  1. 01

    Cook the ground chicken in a pan and set it aside.

  2. 02

    In a bowl, mix the ricotta, a handful of mozzarella, the parmesan, garlic, pesto and eggs until combined.

Layer and bake

  1. 03

    Spread a thin layer of sauce over the bottom of a square baking dish and add a layer of no-boil noodles.

  2. 04

    Spread on some of the ricotta mixture, then a generous layer of mozzarella and some of the chicken. Drizzle with a little sauce.

  3. 05

    Repeat the noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, chicken and sauce layers twice more, for three layers in all. Go easy on the sauce so it lasts.

  4. 06

    Finish with a layer of noodles, a thick covering of mozzarella and a sprinkle of Italian seasoning.

  5. 07

    Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes, until bubbly and golden.

Notes from the video

About the amounts. The video doesn't give measurements, only one clove of garlic, two eggs and two noodles per layer. Use enough to fill your dish.

Sauce swap. Your own favourite white sauce works in place of the jar.
