German Pancake

Six simple ingredients whisked in five minutes, poured over sizzling butter, and baked until the edges puff up tall and golden. A cherished family breakfast that brings everyone running to the table.

  • Tastes Better From Scratch
  • 9 × 13-inch metal baking dish
  • 425°F (218°C)
  • 22–27 minutes

Ingredients

Batter

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • A dash of salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For the pan

  • 5 tablespoons butter

To serve

  • Powdered sugar
  • Syrup

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk together the eggs, milk, flour, salt and vanilla until smooth.

  2. 02

    Put the butter in a 9 × 13-inch metal baking dish and set it in the oven while it preheats to 425°F (218°C), so the butter melts.

  3. 03

    Pour the batter over the hot melted butter.

  4. 04

    Bake for 22 to 27 minutes, until the edges are puffed up and beautifully golden brown.

  5. 05

    Dust generously with powdered sugar, drizzle with syrup, and serve right away.

Notes from the recipe

Watch it rise. It puffs dramatically in the oven and settles as it cools, so bring it straight to the table.

To serve. Fresh strawberries are lovely alongside, as in the original post.
