Apple Streusel Cake

A German Apfelstreuselkuchen: a lemony butter sponge, a ring of tart apple slices, and a cold cinnamon crumble on top. Serve warm or cold, with whipped cream.

  • mydinner.co.uk
  • Serves 12
  • One 26 cm (10-inch) springform
  • 180°C / 356°F
  • 35–45 minutes

Ingredients

Dry

  • 1¼ cups (150 g) flour — UK plain, US all-purpose, German type 405
  • ⅓ cup (50 g) corn flour / cornstarch
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt

Apples

  • 4 tart apples, about 1 kg / 2 lb — Cox, Braeburn, or Granny Smith
  • 2 tsp fresh lemon juice

Batter

  • ½ cup (100 g) butter, at room temperature
  • ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 3 medium eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 tsp lemon zest

Cinnamon streusel

  • ½ cup (100 g) butter, at room temperature
  • 1¼ cups (150 g) flour
  • ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla sugar (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon

Method

Prepare

  1. 01

    Grease the sides of the springform with butter and line the base with baking parchment. Preheat the oven to 180°C / 356°F, top and bottom heat.

  2. 02

    Sift the flour, corn flour, baking powder, and salt into a bowl and set aside.

  3. 03

    Peel, core, and slice the apples. Toss them in the lemon juice so they don’t brown.

  4. 04

    For the streusel, combine the sugar, vanilla sugar, cinnamon, and butter in a medium bowl. Stir in the flour with a rubber spatula until thick and crumbly. Chill until needed.

Assemble and bake

  1. 05

    Beat the butter and sugar at high speed until smooth and creamy, about 2 minutes. Add the eggs and mix for a further minute, then the lemon zest. Add the flour mixture and mix on low until just combined.

  2. 06

    Spread the batter evenly in the tin.

  3. 07

    Arrange the apple slices in circles over the batter.

  4. 08

    Take the streusel from the fridge and crumble it over the cake with your fingers.

  5. 09

    Bake for 35 to 45 minutes, until a skewer pushed into the middle comes out clean.

  6. 10

    Keep at room temperature until serving. Dust with icing sugar if you like.

Notes from the recipe

From the recipe. Use tart apples — Cox, Braeburn, or Granny Smith.

Keep it cold. Chill the streusel until the last moment, then crumble it on by hand.

To serve. Warm or cold, with a dollop of freshly whipped cream.
