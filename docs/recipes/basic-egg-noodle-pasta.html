Basic Egg Noodle Pasta

Fresh egg pasta made easy with the stand mixer and pasta roller. Four eggs, flour, a splash of water and salt become silky noodles that cook in just a few minutes.

  • KitchenAid pasta roller booklet
  • Makes about 1¼ pounds (579 g) dough
  • Rests 20 minutes

Ingredients

Dough

  • 4 large eggs
  • 3½ cups (830 mL) sifted all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbs (15 mL) water
  • 1 tsp (2 mL) salt

For cooking

  • 6 qt (5.7 L) water
  • 2 tsp (10 mL) salt
  • 1 tbs (15 mL) oil (optional)

Method

Make the dough

  1. 01

    Break the eggs into a glass measuring cup. They should measure ⅞ cup (207 mL); if not, add water 1 tsp (2 mL) at a time until they do.

  2. 02

    Put the flour and salt in the stand mixer bowl and attach the flat beater. On Speed 2, gradually add the eggs and 1 tbs (15 mL) water. Mix for 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Switch to the dough hook and knead on Speed 2 for 2 minutes.

  4. 04

    Let the dough rest for 20 minutes, then divide into 4 pieces and run through the pasta roller.

Cook

  1. 05

    Bring 6 qt (5.7 L) of water to a boil with the salt and oil, if using.

  2. 06

    Gradually add the pasta and boil until al dente, stirring occasionally since it floats. Fresh pasta takes 2 to 5 minutes depending on thickness (dry pasta about 7).

  3. 07

    Drain in a colander.

From the booklet

Food safety. Don't leave anything made with eggs, dairy or meat unrefrigerated for more than an hour.
