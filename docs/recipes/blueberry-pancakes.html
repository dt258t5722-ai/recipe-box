Blueberry Pancakes

Thick, fluffy pancakes with the blueberries dropped in as they cook. Cinnamon and maple flavouring to taste.

  • Handwritten card
  • About ½ cup batter each
  • Hot pan

Ingredients

Dry

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1–2 tbsp white sugar
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt

Wet

  • 2 eggs
  • 1¾ cups milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

To taste

  • Cinnamon
  • Maple flavouring
  • Blueberries

Method

  1. 01

    Beat the eggs. Beat in the milk, vegetable oil, and maple flavouring.

  2. 02

    Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon to the liquid.

  3. 03

    Beat lightly, just until combined.

  4. 04

    Heat the pan to high and grease lightly with shortening. Use about ½ cup batter for each pancake.

  5. 05

    Drop blueberries into each pancake as the first side cooks. Once the first side is golden, flip and cook the second side.

From the original card

From the card. If using frozen blueberries, cook a little longer to make sure the blueberries are thawed.
